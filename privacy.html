Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Pausive is provided by Bohdan Diachenko, an independent developer. For privacy questions, contact dbappsupport@gmail.com.

Your information in Pausive

Pausive helps you pause before making an in-app purchase and reflect on decisions you choose to record. It does not require a Pausive account. Your journal entries, notes, amounts and currencies, reflections, personal reasons, preferences, and pause or purchase-window settings are stored in the app's local storage.

Pausive uses this information to display your saved entries and progress, remember your preferences, and operate the features you choose. The developer does not receive your journal or personal notes automatically. Pausive has no developer-run backend for storing this information and includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

Screen Time, notifications and Live Activities

If you authorize Screen Time access, Pausive uses Apple's public Screen Time APIs to apply supported in-app purchase restrictions and manage pauses and temporary purchase windows. Authorization and local session information are used for those features. You can manage authorization in iOS Settings.

Pausive cannot read products, prices, checkout attempts, completed transactions, or purchase history in other apps. Amounts and outcomes shown in your journal are information you choose to enter, not a verified record of purchases in other apps.

If you enable notifications or Live Activities, iOS can display reminders or timer status, including on the Lock Screen. Pausive offers private notification previews; you can also manage notifications and Live Activities through iOS Settings. Pausive schedules local notifications and does not operate a remote push server.

Purchases of Pausive Lifetime

Apple processes the optional Pausive Lifetime purchase through the App Store. Pausive uses Apple's StoreKit framework to obtain product information and verify purchase entitlement, including restoration of access. Pausive does not receive your payment card details. Apple's processing of App Store transactions is governed by Apple's privacy policies and terms.

Export, backups and external services

Pausive can create a readable summary or data archive when you request an export. The contents depend on the export you choose; an archive can include saved entries, reflections, notes, and settings. You choose where to save or share the file using iOS. The developer does not automatically receive the exported file. A service you choose to save or share it with handles that file under its own practices.

Pausive does not provide its own cloud account or journal-sync service. Depending on your device settings, iOS or computer backups may include app data. Those backups are managed through Apple or your backup software, not a Pausive server.

Retention and deletion

Your local entries remain available until you delete them or reset the app's data. You can delete individual journal entries or all journal entries. Deleting journal entries does not also delete weekly reflections or preferences. The separate Reset all Pausive data action removes local journal entries, weekly reflections, preferences, and session state and returns to onboarding.

Deleting or resetting local data does not delete copies you previously exported, files held by services you selected, or backups outside Pausive. Manage those copies separately. A local data reset does not cancel the App Store transaction or erase Apple's purchase records. System permissions are managed in iOS Settings.

Contacting the developer

If you choose to email dbappsupport@gmail.com, the developer receives the email address, message, and attachments you send and uses them to answer your request. Please share only what is needed to explain the issue; your private journal is not required for support.

Support correspondence is handled through Gmail, provided by Google. Messages are used to answer your request and resolve related support issues, not for advertising. Correspondence is retained only as long as needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations. You can request its deletion at the address above. Google's processing is described in its Privacy Policy.

This website and external providers

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms, or embedded third-party fonts. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting processing is described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

External providers such as Google and GitHub may process information in countries outside your own, as described in their policies. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning information supplied in support correspondence. Contact dbappsupport@gmail.com to make a request. The developer cannot access local journal information that you have not shared.

Policy updates

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new revision date. Contact dbappsupport@gmail.com with questions about this policy.
