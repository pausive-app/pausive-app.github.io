Pausive Support

Help with taking a pause, reflecting on a decision, and using Pausive on your device.

Contact the developer

dbappsupport@gmail.com

You can open the link in your email app or copy the address. For a technical issue, include your Pausive version, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the issue.

A screenshot can help if you are comfortable sharing it. Remove private notes or other personal information first. Your journal, payment details, and Apple Account credentials are not needed for support.

Restore Pausive Lifetime

Use Restore purchase in Pausive while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase. The App Store checks whether that account has the Lifetime purchase.

Resetting Pausive data does not cancel the purchase. If access cannot be confirmed, contact support with the message you see.

Screen Time and purchase protection

Protection requires Screen Time authorization and uses the restrictions available through Apple's public APIs. You can inspect protection status in Pausive and manage authorization in iOS Settings.

Pausive cannot read another app's products, prices, or completed transactions and cannot guarantee that another app's purchase flow will open. Follow the status shown by Pausive and iOS.

Your saved information

Journal entries and reflections are stored locally. You can export information, delete entries, or reset local app data in Settings → Data & privacy. Backups and previously exported files are managed separately.

The developer cannot retrieve your private journal from a Pausive server. Read the Privacy Policy for details about local storage, Apple services, exports, and support correspondence.
